THE TRAINING PROCESS

How the training works

  1. 01

    Choose a course

    Compare the syllabus with your current skills and the roles you want to apply for.

  2. 02

    Learn the core subjects

    Study the tools and concepts in your course, then practise them through exercises.

  3. 03

    Complete projects

    Use what you have learned to build projects you can explain in a portfolio or interview.

  4. 04

    Prepare for interviews

    Work on your portfolio and practise explaining your approach to technical problems.

  5. 05

    Apply for suitable roles

    Use your skills and project work when applying for jobs or exploring a role change at your company.

Learning paths and support vary by program. Individual outcomes depend on background, effort and opportunities; employment is not guaranteed.
